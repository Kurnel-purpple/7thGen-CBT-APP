Term Broadsheet

A class-wide performance sheet for a term: every student's CA, exam score, and total per subject, with their overall total, percentage, and position in the class. Compile the 3rd Term and the sheet also carries each student's session performance — 1st, 2nd and 3rd term totals, the section total, and the session average, percentage and position.
